Falling Sand Game — a real physics sandbox, right in your browser

Falling Sand Game is a free online falling sand simulator: draw with sand, water, fire, oil, acid, lava, ice and plants, then watch a real cellular-automaton physics engine take over. Every grain reacts to gravity, density, heat and wind exactly like a proper dropping sand game should — no plugin, no install, no account, just an empty canvas and whatever you want to build, melt or burn down.

This is more than a single falling sand toy. Falling Sand Game 2 adds wind and adjustable simulation speed so you can watch falling sands fast or in gentle slow motion, while Falling Sand Game 3 introduces people, stick figures, zombies and a dedicated Pyro mode for chaotic fire physics. If you grew up with the original falling sand game — or discovered dropping sand games like it through Artsology's sand garden — this is the same idea, rebuilt from scratch with more materials, better performance and full mobile touch support.

FAQ

Everything about Falling Sand Game

What is a falling sand game?

A falling sand game is a physics sandbox built on a cellular automaton: every grain on screen follows simple rules for gravity, density and reaction, and those small rules add up to realistic-looking sand, water and fire behavior. You place particles and watch the simulation take over.

Is Falling Sand Game free to play online?

Yes. Falling Sand Game is completely free to play online in any modern browser, on desktop or mobile, with no account, no download and no install required.

Do I need to download the falling sand game?

No download is needed. The entire simulation runs live on this page using HTML5 canvas, so there is nothing to install. You can, however, download a PNG snapshot of any sand art you create using the Save button in the toolbar.

Can I play this falling sand game unblocked at school or work?

Because the game runs entirely in the browser with no downloads, installs or plugins, it is generally easy to open on shared or restricted computers during breaks, as long as your local network allows browser games.

What is the difference between Falling Sand Game 2 and Falling Sand Game 3?

Falling Sand Game 2 expands the original sandbox with wind physics, adjustable simulation speed, and reactive liquids like oil, acid and lava. Falling Sand Game 3 goes further with People mode, Zombie mode, Pyro mode, Art mode and an experimental 3D shading mode — all inside the same simulator, switchable from one toolbar.

Does the game support wind and fast falling sand speeds?

Yes. A wind slider pushes every particle sideways in real time, and a simulation-speed control lets you switch between slow, normal, fast and turbo so sand, water and smoke fall and settle faster.

Can I play with stick figures, people or zombies?

Yes. Falling Sand Game 3 adds a Person (stick-figure) particle and a Zombie particle. Drop zombies near a crowd of people and watch the infection spread, or burn them away with fire instead.

Is there a 3D falling sand game mode?

3D Mode adds ambient shading and edge highlights to every particle so sand piles, water pools and stone walls read with real depth and dimension, without leaving the browser or needing a dedicated 3D engine.

How does this compare to other dropping sand games, like the Artsology sand game?

Like the Artsology sand game and other classic dropping sand games, you draw directly onto a live canvas. This version adds more materials, a dedicated Art mode for colorful sand painting, wind and speed controls, and full mobile touch support.

Will there be a falling sand game download for offline play?

Not currently — the game is designed to be an instant falling sand game online with zero install friction. The Save PNG button covers the most common reason people look for a download: keeping a copy of what they built.

About Us

Built by people who obsess over particle physics

Falling Sand Game is maintained by a small team focused on one thing: making the most satisfying, most stable dropping sand game on the web.
